Quotation assets
================

Golos Text is bundled under the SIL Open Font License in fonts/OFL.txt.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/golostext
Static instances at weights 400, 700 and 900 were generated from the official
variable font; their subfamily and PostScript names identify their actual weight.
No font download or installed Windows font is needed at runtime.

led-profi-logo.png is the company mark extracted from the reference quotation
supplied by the application owner. It includes only the mark, without customer
information or other contents of that quotation. Its original proportions are
preserved. A user-supplied company logo takes precedence.
